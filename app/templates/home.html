How a report gets made

The model describes; it never decides. Every verdict comes from plain rules you can read, applied to what was actually in the photos.

What a report is, and what it isn't

It contrasts claims with photo evidence and says what stays inconclusive. It never certifies the item or the seller.

It is

  • A record of what specific photos showed about specific claims, on one version of the listing.
  • A signal against reused or downloaded photos, when the listing's code is read in a usable photo.
  • Tamper-evident: a fingerprint covers the report and its photos.

It isn't

  • Proof of ownership, current possession or authenticity.
  • A check of function, full condition or the seller's trustworthiness. It never scores people.
  • Aware of edits made on Marketplace itself; only edits in Verified Asset void a report.
